snoobytalk — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

No data is collected. No data is shared. Nothing leaves your device.

What snoobytalk does

snoobytalk is a novelty app that plays back sounds and reactions in response to your dog's bark or your voice, for entertainment purposes. It works entirely offline: all art, sounds, and app logic are bundled into the app itself, and no part of the app communicates with the internet.

Microphone use

snoobytalk asks for microphone permission so you can trigger sounds by speaking or letting your dog bark near the device. Audio is processed live by your device's own built-in speech recognition system (on most Android phones, this is the operating system's speech service). snoobytalk does not record, store, upload, or transmit any audio. Nothing you or your dog say is saved beyond the moment it's used to trigger a sound, and no transcript or recording ever leaves your device.

Data we collect

None. snoobytalk does not collect personal information, device identifiers, location data, contacts, or usage analytics. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, and no advertising SDKs in the app.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps a short history of recent interactions (for example, recently triggered sounds) saved locally on your device so you can replay them. This history never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings.

Data sharing

snoobytalk does not share any data with third parties, because it does not collect any data to share. There are no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking libraries included in the app.

Children's privacy

snoobytalk does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future update adds a feature that uses data differently — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
